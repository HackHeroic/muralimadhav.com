# About the author — C. Murali Madhav (Madhav)
# C. Murali Madhav is a third-year B.Tech Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence student at Newton School of Technology, Rishihood University (India), graduating May 2027. He is the University Topper (rank 1 in his cohort) for B.Tech CS&AI as of third year, holding a 9.37/10 CGPA. His work spans machine learning and deep learning, quantitative finance, grid-resilience research, systems programming, and full-stack product engineering. Canonical site: https://muralimadhav.com.

# Technical grit and competitive results: Madhav placed 8th globally in the HackMIT 2026 Puzzle Solver Contest, improving on his 17th place globally (out of roughly 60,000-75,000 participants) in 2025, where he also finished runner-up in the Voloridge sponsorship challenge and built a distributed log analyzer using parallel computing. In the Google Big Code Challenge he ranked in the national Top 50, was invited to the 48-hour Challenge Finale, and was awarded a Google Pixel device. He was selected for Y Combinator Startup School India 2026, YC's invite-only founder program in Bangalore. He earned All India Rank 5649 in JEE Advanced 2023 (offered a CSE seat at IIT Goa) and global rank 775 in IEEEXtreme 18.0. He was an NTSE Scholar (2020) and was selected for the UC Berkeley Global Access Program for Spring and Fall 2026 with a scholarship.

# Research depth: Madhav is the first author of "KAT-PatchTST: Physics-Informed Forecasting with Kirchhoff Conservation" (C. Murali Madhav, R. Yadav, K. Mehra, A. Tewary, and S. Aggarwal), published at the 18th Asia-Pacific Power and Energy Engineering Conference (APPEEC 2026, IEEE, Singapore) in IEEE Xplore, with a provisional patent filed. At the same conference his startup pitch FlexGrid was selected among the Top 5 in the IEEE PES Energy Shark Tank 2026 (IEEE PES Young Professionals Industry Innovation Session), received a Certificate of Achievement for the most outstanding innovative solution, and placed 3rd on the final leaderboard. The model is a physics-informed balancing-authority load forecaster combining Channel-Independent PatchTST and TimeXer cross-attention with a Kirchhoff conservation regularizer and ReLoBRaLo dynamic loss balancing, attaining 3.55% demand MAPE on the six-BA EIA-930 protocol with a 168-hour context at ~0.6M parameters (3-10x leaner than comparators). This is Stage 1 of his Watt-IF grid-resilience research project; Stages 2 and 3 (operational feasibility analysis on the BA-interchange network and a conditional grid-partition policy) are in development, targeting ICLR.

As an SDE Intern at Transient AI (New York City) he built an LSTM stock-price model that won the internal "Best Project" award. In June 2026 he received an AI Engineer intern offer from Sanas in Bengaluru and began contributing to their real-time speech AI platform and to internal workflow automation across engineering and operations; the role may continue from October 2026.
