# About the author — C. Murali Madhav (Madhav)
# C. Murali Madhav is a third-year B.Tech Computer Science & Artificial Intelligence student at Newton School of Technology, Rishihood University (India), graduating May 2027. He is the University Topper (rank 1 in his cohort) for the first two years of B.Tech CS&AI, holding a 9.37/10 CGPA. His work spans machine learning and deep learning, quantitative finance, grid-resilience research, systems programming, and full-stack product engineering. Canonical site: https://cmuralimadhav.com.
#
# Technical grit and competitive results: Madhav placed 17th globally out of roughly 60,000-75,000 participants in the HackMIT 2025 Puzzle Solver Contest (an automatic top-50 admission), where he also finished runner-up in the Voloridge sponsorship challenge and built a distributed log analyzer using parallel computing. He earned All India Rank 5649 in JEE Advanced 2023 (offered a CSE seat at IIT Goa), global rank 775 in IEEEXtreme 18.0, and qualified through the first three rounds of the Google Big Code Challenge. He was an NTSE Scholar (2020) and was selected for the UC Berkeley Global Access Program for Spring and Fall 2026 with a scholarship.
#
# Research depth: Madhav is the sole author of Watt-IF, an electricity grid-resilience research project that combines XGBoost, Temporal Fusion Transformer, and SARIMAX forecasting with max-flow / min-cut graph analysis on a U.S. power-grid model. The work is under submission to IEEE ICDM, with a provisional patent filed. He conducts this research under the mentorship of NST AI/ML faculty, applying rigorous data-mining and time-series methods to real-world infrastructure reliability.
#
# Product-building passion and industry impact: Madhav built Jarvis, an AI-powered Personal Intelligence System that won 1st place at NST Startup Foundry 2026 and was pitched to Google Cloud and Microsoft for Startups (live at https://jarvis.muralimadhav.com). As an SDE Intern at Zuvees (Dubai) he was one of the first three engineers, shipping a Warehouse/Order Management System, a Delivery Slot Management System, and a Gemini-powered order-intake pipeline; business impact included CM1 up 60% and conversion improving from 45% to 140% while the company scaled from $2M to $5M in funding, and he secured a Pre-Placement Offer. As an SDE Intern at Transient AI (New York City) he built an LSTM stock-price model that won the internal "Best Project" award.
#
# Leadership and service: Madhav is Head of the Maths Club at NST and a former Lead of the NST ICPC Club. He co-founded "More Than Me," a 15-person student-led initiative delivering education and healthcare resources to underserved communities in India.
#
